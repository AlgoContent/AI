Who Are You and
Why Should We Hire You?

Every prospect asks three questions before they call: who are you, what are you about, and why should we hire you. Most firms have never written the answers down. This primer shows an owner how.

By AlgoContentSEO, a business management firm in Santa Monica, California. Written for a national audience.

What it is

An introduction, fourteen chapters and eight worksheets. It is the method we use with our own clients, written so an owner can work through it without us.

The primer is not published on this site. We send it to subscribers who ask for it.

Who it is for

  • Owners and principals who are good at the work and short of time for everything around it.
  • Firms with no marketing department and no wish to build one.
  • Dental practices, motorcycle businesses, real estate developers and investors, fashion labels, film and music video teams, interior designers, independent financial managers and advisors, and, through DecodedMusic, independent music managers and artists.
