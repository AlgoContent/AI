What we do

One manager for the work around the work.

Most owners do not need another vendor. They need someone who knows the business, sits in the weekly conversation and makes sure things happen. That is the job. We agree a list, we do what is on it, and we tell you plainly what it is doing for you.

  • Business management.The recurring work that keeps an owner from the work only the owner can do: schedules, follow-up, vendors, approvals and reporting.
  • Marketing.Your position in plain words, then a website, a one-page summary, a deck and email that all say the same thing.
  • PR.The story around a release, a launch, a project or the firm itself. The media list, the pitches, the interview preparation and the follow-up.
  • Social media.A calendar you approve, the writing, the scheduling and a monthly look at what worked.
  • Search and content.Both are in our name. Articles and pages built from the questions your clients ask, and the technical side of being found.
  • Capital-raising materials.Decks, summaries and investor updates, organized within what your counsel allows.

About AlgoContentSEO

Three questions, answered about ourselves.

Every prospect asks three things before they call: who are you, what are you about, and why should we hire you. It would be odd to ask our clients to answer them and not do it ourselves.

Who are we?

AlgoContentSEO is a business management firm in Santa Monica, California. We work across the country with owners who are very good at their own craft and stretched thin by everything around it. Most have no marketing department and no wish to build one.

What are we about?

We believe marketing and operations belong together. Owners fall behind on marketing because they are buried in other work, so we help with both.

We believe a firm should be able to explain itself in plain words. A firm that cannot say who it serves usually has trouble with pricing, hiring and focus as well.

We believe in honest claims. We will not write something about your firm that you cannot stand behind. In regulated fields we work alongside your counsel and compliance staff.

We believe the work should be yours. Your website, your accounts and your contact list belong to you from the first day.

Why hire us?

Because we work as part of your team. We keep a weekly rhythm with you, one person on your side approves what goes out, and nothing is published in your name without your say. You get a manager who does the work, where an agency would hand you a report about it.

Working together

How we join your team.

  1. A first conversation. We learn where you are and whether we are a fit. If we are not, we will say so.
  2. Discovery. We talk with the principals, review what exists and look at the firms you are compared with. You receive a written summary.
  3. A written scope. We agree what you will receive, who approves it and when. Nothing is left to memory.
  4. The work. We build, write, schedule and follow up. You give us about an hour a week and approve everything that goes out.
  5. Review. At ninety days we look at what was done and what changed, and decide together what continues.

What we do not promise

We do not promise rankings, press coverage or a number of new clients. Nobody can honestly promise those. We promise clear work, delivered on schedule, and reporting that tells you the truth about what is working.
