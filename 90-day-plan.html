The 90-Day Plan · $10,000 fixed fee

People look you up before they call. In 90 days, give them something worth finding.

The 90-Day Plan is one fixed fee for a fixed list of work. We review your website and your social pages, write your content, and sit down with your team every month. You know what you will receive before you pay.

The ten minutes you are not there for

Someone hears your name from a client. Before they pick up the phone, they search. They read your website on a phone, glance at your profile and compare you with two other firms.

It takes them about ten minutes. If what they find is thin, out of date or sounds like every other firm, they move on. Nobody tells you. The phone is just a little quieter.

You probably know this already. What you do not have is a free afternoon to fix it.

Hand us the afternoon

For 90 days we do the reviewing, the writing and the scheduling. You keep the three things that need you: your point of view, your relationships and the final say on everything that goes out.

What you receive

  • Website review

    A written report on up to 15 pages of your site, with the fixes in priority order.

  • Social media review

    A written report on up to three profiles and your last 90 days of posts.

  • Your three answers

    One page that says who you are, what you are about and why someone should hire you.

  • Five web pages rewritten

    Your core pages, up to 400 words each, built from your three answers.

  • Twelve pieces of content

    Six articles, three client letters and three sets of eight social posts, on a calendar you approve.

  • Three discussions with your team

    One hour each month, then a closing report and a plan for your next quarter.

How the 90 days run

  1. Days 1 to 30

    Review and rebuild

    Both reviews, your three answers, five rewritten pages and the first four pieces.

  2. Days 31 to 60

    Publish

    Four more pieces go out on schedule.

  3. Days 61 to 90

    Measure and plan

    Four more pieces, the report and the plan for your next quarter.

One round of changes on every piece

Every piece comes with one round of changes. Send us one list within five business days and we turn it around. Your three answers get two rounds, because everything else is built on them.

If we do not hear from you in five business days, we take the piece as approved and keep moving. That keeps your 90 days on schedule.

Work beyond the list, or a change of direction after you have approved something, is quoted at $175 an hour before we start. You will never get a surprise bill.

What we will not promise

We will not promise you a ranking, a headline or a number of leads. Nobody controls those. We promise the work on the list, on schedule, and a plain report on what it is doing for you.

If you work in a regulated field, nothing goes out until your compliance reviewer or counsel has approved it. We build that step into the calendar.

Questions

How much of my time will this take?
About an hour a week. One opening conversation, a monthly discussion and your approvals.
Who owns the work?
You do, once the fee is paid in full. Your site, your accounts and your contact list stay yours from the first day.
What happens after 90 days?
You receive a plan for the next quarter. Run it yourself or keep us on. Nothing renews unless you ask.
Can I stop early?
Yes, by writing to us. Payments already made cover work already done, and no further payments are taken.
Do you work outside California?
Yes. We are in Santa Monica and work with firms nationwide.
