Industries

The fields we work in.

An outsourced business manager is only useful if they understand how your kind of business earns its living. These are the fields where we do.

By AlgoContentSEO · Santa Monica, California · Working nationwide

We began with dental practices, motorcycle businesses and real estate. With new staff on the team we have added fashion labels, film and music video, interior designers, independent music through DecodedMusic, and independent financial managers.

The work is the same in every field. We take on the recurring tasks around your craft, and we handle marketing, PR and social media as part of running the business. What changes is the detail: who your client is, what they check before they call, what you are allowed to say, and when your busy season leaves you no time to say it. Each page below goes into that detail.

  • Dental practices

    The dentist belongs in the operatory. We run the business side, from follow-up lists to the website, reviews and social media.

    Business management for dental practices ↗

  • Motorcycle businesses

    Riding season does not wait for your marketing. We keep the shop visible while your crew is busy on the floor and in the bays.

    Business management for motorcycle shops ↗

  • Real estate

    Investors and partners look you up before the first meeting. We keep the website, the project stories and the deck ready all year.

    Business management for real estate firms ↗

  • Fashion labels

    The collection is ready. The line sheet, the press note and the posts are not. We run the business side as part of your studio.

    Business management for fashion labels ↗

  • Film and music video

    A finished film still needs an audience, and a director needs the next job. We run the business side between one project and the next.

    Business management for film and music video ↗

  • Interior designers

    Your best rooms are sitting in a folder, unphotographed and unpublished. We run the business side as part of your studio.

    Business management for interior designers ↗
